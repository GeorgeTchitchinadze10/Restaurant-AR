How it works

Scan. Inspect in 3D. Place it on your table.

No download. It runs in the browser the guest already has open.

STEP 01

Scan the QR code

A code on the table opens the menu instantly — categories, prices, and a live 3D thumbnail of every dish. Nothing to install.

STEP 02

Inspect in 3D

Tap any dish to rotate and zoom a photoreal model. See the real layering and presentation.

STEP 03

Place it on your table

Tap "View on table" and the dish appears at true size on the real table. Works on any iPhone or Android.

FAQ

Questions owners ask us.

Do my guests need to download an app?
No. The menu opens straight from the phone's browser the moment they scan the QR code — nothing to install, for them or for you.
Does it work on iPhone?
Yes — iPhone and Android both. The 3D view and the "place it on your table" AR work on any modern smartphone.
How long does it take to set up?
We come to you, photograph your 5 dishes, and your menu goes live in about two days. You don't touch anything technical.
What happens after setup?
You keep the menu on a simple monthly plan. We host it, and you edit items and prices yourself anytime.
